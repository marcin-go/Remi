## Framework-lifecycle rules

- G-Cloud 13 is reporting-only. Remi must not offer new-contract registration or Marketplace ID suggestions for it; invoices are reported against existing registered contracts.
- G-Cloud 15 is open as a framework choice, but StatMap's public service catalogue and Marketplace IDs have not yet been published. Remi must require the ID shown in the signed contract and must not reuse a G-Cloud 14 ID.
- A product is modelled separately from its framework-versioned Marketplace ID. The same product can have different IDs under G-Cloud 13, G-Cloud 14 and G-Cloud 15.
